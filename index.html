About TechXpo 2.O

TechXpo 2.0 is where innovation meets imagination and technology shapes tomorrow. More than just a tech festival, it's an immersive experience celebrating creativity, emerging technologies. Organized by Circuitron – The IoT & Embedded Systems Club of RVSCET, TechXpo 2.0 brings together passionate innovators, tech enthusiasts, and future engineers to explore ideas, showcase talent, build solutions, and push the boundaries of what's possible.

Next-Gen IoT & Embedded Arena

From autonomous line-followers and heavy combat bots to cutting-edge smart hardware prototypes, TechXpo 2.O offers real-world arena challenges judged by distinguished academia and industry mentors.
