The Story of Circuitron

Founded in 2018, Circuitron has spearheaded embedded systems and IoT innovation at RVS College of Engineering & Technology, Jamshedpur. What originated as a determined group of electronics and robotics enthusiasts has evolved into one of the most proactive technical societies in the region.

Our journey was sparked by a clear objective: bridging the divide between classroom theoretical concepts and applied engineering reality. Over the seasons, we have orchestrated high-impact hands-on bootcamps, competitive hackathons, and state-level robotics challenges.

Today, TechXpo 2.O stands as our premier annual showcase — a vibrant stage where passionate engineers demonstrate autonomous machines, smart systems, and groundbreaking projects.

Why TechXpo 2.O Matters

> For Students & Creators

  • Hands-on arena combat experience against leading regional teams.
  • Direct evaluations and technical feedback from experienced university faculty.
